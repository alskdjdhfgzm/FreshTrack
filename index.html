🌡️ Temperature
—°C

How warm the air inside the container is. Heat speeds up bacteria and mold, so warmer food spoils faster.

💧 Humidity
—%RH

The amount of moisture in the air. Too much encourages mold, and a climb can mean food is breaking down and releasing water.

☁️ eCO2
—ppm

Estimated carbon dioxide, worked out by the sensor from the gases it detects. It rises as microbes and decaying food give off CO₂.

🧪 TVOC
—ppb

Total volatile organic compounds: the smelly gases (alcohols, amines and more) released as food rots. A steady rise is a key spoilage signal.
